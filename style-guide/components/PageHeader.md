The kicker and title that open every inside page.

- Provide: the page title, two to four words, Title Case.
- Kicker is always "TKB DINER" in `kicker` (teal, uppercase, 0.24em tracking); title in `page-title` (maroon, 54px).
- Place it directly under the band, `section-gap` above the content. One per page.
- Classic Breakfast is the one exception: an outlined teal "Served all day" tag (2px `teal` border, 10px radius, `label` type at 13px) sits flush right on the title line.
- Don't: add a subtitle, change the kicker per page, or let the title wrap to two lines (shorten it instead).
