A teal-outlined box for a combo deal or a reference list.

- Provide: either one MenuItem (the Sampler) or a `label` plus a dot-separated list (Dressings).
- 2px `teal` border, `radius-box` corners, 12px × 16px padding, no fill.
- Use for information that applies across a section rather than one dish.
- Don't: put more than one item in it, or fill it with color (that's the HighlightBox).
