A solid dark box that makes one dish or message the star of the page.

- Provide: an item (name, price, description) or a message (label, headline, one line); optionally a small uppercase label for timing ("Last Friday & Saturday of the month").
- Two fills: `ink` for the signature dish or a house message (The Black Squirrel, We Cater) and `maroon` for a time-limited special (Prime Rib). Text in `on-dark`; descriptions in `on-dark-soft` (on ink) or `on-maroon-label` (on maroon); the ink price in `on-dark-price`; labels in `on-dark-label` (on ink) or `on-maroon-label` (on maroon).
- `radius-feature` corners, `callout-pad-y` × `callout-pad-x` padding, item name 22–26px.
- At most two on a page, never side by side.
- Don't: use teal or seasonal colors as a fill, or put a list of items inside.
