The decorative strip across the top of every page.

- Regular menu: the checkerboard, 16px tall (`band-height-checker`), 8px squares of `check-red` and `paper`, `band-width` wide.
- Position: `band-inset-top` (28px) from the top edge and `band-inset-x` (30px) from each side, so it clears the printer's `safe-zone`. On a cover it repeats at the bottom, 28px from the bottom edge.
- Holiday menus swap the checkerboard for a seasonal strip (see Holidays & special events). Everything else on the page stays the same.
- Don't: run the band to the paper edge, stack two bands, or put text over it.
